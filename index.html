[ CHAPTER ZERO: THE FOUNDING SIGNAL ] CLICK TO DECRYPT FULL TRANSMISSION

In the middle of the busy market in the slums of the dark and cloudy Cyrclend City, I was sitting on the floor, trying to cover the bleeding wound on my stomach.

I was used to being treated as invisible, but when you're dying, it carries a heavier weight. Everyone was busy bargaining for weapons, crystals, and clothes. Me, on the other hand, didn't know what hurt more: the bleeding wound on my stomach, which had gotten even worse after being beaten up by the guards who caught me trying to steal some pastries, or the starvation that was making me see double.

After all my friends were killed during our last attempt at a heist, I was the only one who managed to escape. I walked for kilometers through the swamps to reach the downtown market of Cyrclend, only to finally realize it hadn't been worth the effort.

NO FAMILY. NO COMRADES. NO FOOD. NO HOPE.

When I was about to pass out and meet my miserable end, I noticed a pair of eyes on me. Across the street, there was a boy.

A skinny one, probably in his early twenties. White hair, shaved short on the sides. Dark skin, with even darker circles under his eyes. He wore an all-black outfit and a red circle pendant with a curious design around his neck. I could tell he had grown up in the outskirts of Cyrclend City.

He showed no expression, but his eyes told a different story. His red and green pupils seemed like they could pierce a laser straight through my head. I couldn't tell if he was looking down on me or if he was Death himself, coming to reap whatever was left of my soul.

I had no pride left to ask what he was staring at, so I ignored him and looked down at the floor again while another gang fight broke out in the market. I fainted.

I don't know how long I was out, but I woke up when a merchant threw a bucket of cold water on me. Apparently, I was making his business look bad.

My lips were dry from dehydration. My body was too heavy to move. My stomach had shrunk from not eating for days. The blood loss wasn't doing me any favors either. Somehow, I managed to get up, completely drenched.

Then I heard a metallic sound against the floor. Something had fallen from my pocket. It was a key and an envelope. The key was rusty, and the envelope was crumpled.

⭕

"I will give you purpose.
Find me."

Address: Frend Street, 67X — Outskirts of Cyrclend City.

At the end of the message, there was a red circle that looked exactly like the boy's pendant.

"Purpose," he says...

I've got nothing to lose. That is, if I can survive these injuries. The bucket of cold water had worked as a pretty good painkiller. I couldn't feel much anymore. The winters in Cyrclend were always severe.

With the last bit of energy I had left, I made my way to the address the boy had written. The outskirts were like a labyrinth, filled with underground passages and hidden connections. After wandering around for what felt like hours, I finally found a tunnel that led to a very dark, very long corridor.

I noticed the red circle from the envelope drawn as graffiti on the wall I had just passed. Must be this way, I thought.

After I don't know how long, I reached a dead end. There was a wooden door with the word "RENEGADES" written above it. The same circle was drawn around the keyhole.

I could hear noise coming from the other side of the door. "If he brought me here just to steal my organs and sell them, at least I was worth something in the end." That is, if there was still anything worth using...

I used the key to open the door.

The moment I saw what was on the other side, I knew the boy had been right. Later on, I found out his name was Haine.

And my encounter with him that day changed my life. Forever.

[ CHAPTER ONE: THE AWAKENING ]

Decades after the fall, a rogue broadcast emerged from those same underground passages on a frequency no one was supposed to find. It carried music — raw, unfiltered, alive. Those who followed it found each other.

They called themselves RENEG▲DES. Singers, artists, architects of sound and vision. Operatives with no allegiance except to the craft. They built a hidden network — a guild forged in fire and frequency — where every voice matters and "Nobody gets left behind."

Now, from the ashes, they recruit. Not everyone. Only those who are chosen. Only those worthy of a FILE.

01 — WHAT IS RENEGADES?

RENEGADES is not a competition.
It is not a hierarchy built around popularity, talent or activity.
It is a guild.
A place for people to create, collaborate, compete, cause trouble and, most importantly, have fun together.

Members can participate in missions, events and collaborations while earning COFs, the guild's internal currency. Every member has their own role, reputation and progression within the guild. The more you participate and contribute, the more COFs you earn — and the further you can rise within RENEGADES.

There are rules. There are missions. There are rewards.

But above everything else, there is one principle:
Nobody gets left behind.

02 — THE FOUNDERS

The five people who established RENEGADES are Nezumi, Putra, Mamiki, Ten & Sam.

The Founders are the highest authority within the guild and are responsible for keeping RENEGADES organized, active and true to its original purpose. They have:

  • Unlimited COFs
  • Final authority over group decisions
  • Ability to create missions
  • Ability to issue rewards
  • Ability to invite new members
  • Ability to approve and manage events
  • Ability to modify guild regulations
  • Ability to resolve conflicts and disciplinary matters

The Founders may delegate responsibilities to other members when necessary, but final authority always remains with the Founders.

03 — JOINING RENEGADES

RENEGADES follows an Invitation-only system, only the 5 founders have the ability of recruiting new members.

Once accepting the invitation, the recruiter must add all information required in the RENEGADES DATABASE.

Every new member starts at the lowest available rank and can progress through the guild by participating in missions, events and collaborations.

Members will eventually have an individual ranking within RENEGADES. Promotions will be primarily based on the amount of COFs earned, alongside activity and contribution to the guild.

04 — THE RENEGADE’S BANK - COFs CURRENCY

COFs are the official currency of RENEGADES.
COFs represent a member's activity and contribution to the guild. They can be earned through missions, events, collaborations and other activities approved by the Founders.

COFs can be earned by:

  • Completing missions
  • Participating in events
  • Winning or placing in competitions
  • Organizing approved events
  • Helping with guild projects
  • Creating scripts, covers, artwork or other creative material
  • Managing collaborations
  • Performing tasks assigned by the Founders
  • Receiving special rewards or bonuses

COFs can be spent on:

  • Joining collaborations
  • Special guild activities
  • Exclusive rewards
  • Event-related benefits

The Bookkeeper Sam will be responsible for maintaining the official COF balance and may introduce new ways to earn or spend COFs.

COFs have no real-world monetary value and cannot be exchanged for actual money.

05 — MISSIONS

Missions are tasks created to encourage members to participate, contribute and keep the guild active.

Missions can involve almost anything related to RENEGADES and its activities, including:

  • Writing a script
  • Creating cover art
  • Managing a collaboration
  • Joining Chorus Battles
  • Managing the server
  • Organizing documents
  • Creating promotional material
  • Participating in special guild activities

Every mission must clearly state:

  • Mission objective
  • Requirements
  • Deadline
  • COF reward
  • Any additional rules or restrictions

Mission rewards are determined by the person creating the mission and require Founder approval.

06 — EVENTS

Events are special activities organized within RENEGADES. They can be either internal or external.

Events may include:

  • Chorus Battles
  • Internal Battles
  • Daily missions
  • Seasonal activities
  • Themed events

Events must last a minimum of 3 days and a maximum of 30 days.

All members are allowed to propose, create and manage events, provided that the event is approved by the Founders before being officially announced.

The event organizer is responsible for:

  • Preparing the event
  • Explaining the rules
  • Managing participants
  • Keeping track of results
  • Distributing approved COF rewards
  • Reporting the final results to the Founders

Founders may modify, pause or cancel an event if necessary.

07 — COLLABORATION SYSTEM

Collaborations are one of the main activities within RENEGADES.

Members may create collaborations with other members by proposing a project and establishing its requirements before production begins. Depending on the type and scale of the collaboration, participating members may be required to pay a specific amount of COFs.

The collaboration creator must clearly state:

  • The project concept
  • Number of participants
  • Deadline
  • Any additional requirements

COF costs must be approved by the Founders before the collaboration officially begins.

Members who participate in a collaboration must fulfill their assigned role and respect the established deadline. Failure to complete an agreed role without a valid reason may result in the loss of the paid COFs and/or additional consequences.

08 — RANKING & PROMOTIONS

Every RENEGADES member has a rank within the guild.
Ranks represent a member's progression, contribution and activity within RENEGADES.

Promotions are primarily determined by the amount of COFs accumulated, but the Founders may also consider: Activity, Reliability, Participation in events, Contribution to guild projects, Collaboration with other members, Leadership, and Overall conduct.

Accumulating COFs does not automatically guarantee a promotion if a member's behavior or activity does not meet the expectations of their rank. The Founders are responsible for confirming promotions and may create, remove or adjust ranks when necessary. A member's rank may also be reduced if they become inactive or repeatedly violate guild regulations.

09 — GUILD RULES

Every member is expected to respect the following rules:

  • CONFIDENTIALITY: Private discussions, internal decisions and personal information shared within RENEGADES should remain within the guild unless permission is given to share them.
  • NO DRAMA: Personal disagreements should not be turned into public guild conflicts.

If a problem cannot be resolved privately, members should contact a Founder.

10 — CODE OF CONDUCT

INACTIVITY

RENEGADES IS A HOBBY, NOT A JOB.
Missions, events, COFS and rankings exist to give the group something fun to interact with. Nobody is expected to be constantly active. Nobody is expected to participate in every event. Nobody loses their place because real life got busy.

The system exists to reward participation — not punish absence. Members are allowed to take breaks when needed. However, if someone expects to be inactive for an extended period, they should inform a Founder whenever possible.

LEAVING RENEGADES

Members are free to leave RENEGADES at any time. Leaving the guild is not considered an offense and members do not need to justify their decision.

However, upon leaving:

  • Membership privileges are removed
  • Unused COFs are forfeited
  • Active RENEGADES roles may be reassigned
  • Ongoing responsibilities should be completed or transferred whenever possible

Returning to RENEGADES is possible, but requires a new invitation from a Founder.

FOUNDER AUTHORITY

The Founders reserve the right to intervene in situations not explicitly covered by these rules. If a situation clearly goes against the spirit of RENEGADES, the Founders may take appropriate action even if the exact situation is not specifically listed in the regulations.
