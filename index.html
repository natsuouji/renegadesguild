// TRANSMISSION INTERCEPT

The networks fell silent decades ago. What remains is a fractured digital wasteland — abandoned frequencies, dead servers, corrupted archives. Governments dissolved. Corporations burned. Art was outlawed.

But in the static, a signal emerged. A rogue broadcast on a frequency no one was supposed to find. It carried music — raw, unfiltered, alive. Those who followed it found each other.

They called themselves RENEG▲DES. Singers, artists, architects of sound and vision. Operatives with no allegiance except to the craft. They built a hidden network — a guild forged in fire and frequency — where every voice matters and "Nobody gets left behind."

Now, from the ashes, they recruit. Not everyone. Only those who are chosen. Only those worthy of a FILE.
