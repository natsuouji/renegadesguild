GUILD MANIFESTO

01WHAT IS RENEGADES?[+]

RENEGADES is not a competition. It is not a hierarchy built around popularity, talent or activity.

It is a guild.
A place for people to create, collaborate, compete, cause trouble and, most importantly, have fun together.

Members can participate in missions, events and collaborations while earning COFs, the guild's internal currency. Every member has their own role, reputation and progression within the guild. The more you participate and contribute, the more COFs you earn — and the further you can rise within RENEGADES.

There are rules. There are missions. There are rewards. But above everything else, there is one principle:
Nobody gets left behind.

02THE FOUNDERS[+]

The five people who established RENEGADES are Nezumi, Ouji, Ten, Mamiki & Sam.
The Founders are the highest authority within the guild and are responsible for keeping RENEGADES organized, active and true to its original purpose.

  • Unlimited COFs
  • Final authority over group decisions
  • Ability to create missions & issue rewards
  • Ability to invite new members
  • Ability to approve and manage events
  • Ability to modify guild regulations
  • Ability to resolve conflicts and disciplinary matters

The Founders may delegate responsibilities to other members when necessary, but final authority always remains with the Founders.

03JOINING RENEGADES[+]

RENEGADES follows an Invitation-only system. Only the 5 founders have the ability to recruit new members.

Once accepting the invitation, the recruiter must add all information required in the RENEGADES DATABASE. Every new member starts at the lowest available rank and can progress through the guild by participating in missions, events and collaborations.

04THE RENEGADE’S BANK (COFs)[+]

COFs are the official currency of RENEGADES.
They represent a member's activity and contribution to the guild. They have no real-world monetary value.

HOW TO EARN COFs:

  • Completing missions / Participating in events
  • Winning or placing in competitions
  • Organizing approved events / Managing collabs
  • Creating scripts, covers, artwork or creative material
  • Helping with guild projects or tasks assigned by Founders

HOW TO SPEND COFs:

  • Joining collaborations
  • Special guild activities & Event-related benefits
  • Exclusive rewards

The Bookkeeper Sam will be responsible for maintaining the official COF balance.

05MISSIONS & EVENTS[+]

MISSIONS: Tasks created to encourage participation. Can involve writing scripts, creating cover art, managing collabs, joining Chorus Battles, managing the server, etc.

Every mission must clearly state: Objective, Requirements, Deadline, COF Reward, and Restrictions. Rewards require Founder approval.

EVENTS: Internal or external special activities (Chorus Battles, Daily Missions, Seasonal activities). Must last 3 to 30 days. All members can propose events. The organizer prepares rules, manages participants, tracks results, and distributes approved COFs.

06COLLABORATION SYSTEM[+]

Collaborations are one of the main activities within RENEGADES. Members may create collaborations by proposing a project.

Depending on the scale, participants may be required to pay a specific amount of COFs. The creator must state the concept, participant limit, and deadline. COF costs must be Founder-approved.

Failure to complete an agreed role without a valid reason may result in the loss of paid COFs and additional consequences.

07RULES & CONDUCT[+]

CONFIDENTIALITY: Private discussions and internal decisions should remain within the guild.
NO DRAMA: Personal disagreements should not be public. Contact a Founder if needed.

INACTIVITY:

RENEGADES IS A HOBBY, NOT A JOB. The system exists to reward participation — not punish absence. Members can take breaks. Inform a Founder if leaving for an extended period.

LEAVING RENEGADES:

Members are free to leave anytime without justification. Privileges are removed, unused COFs forfeited. Returning requires a new invite.
